Conversation marking is a <span className="enterprise-field-badge">Commercial</span> concept, but the frozen `0.2.0-rc.3` contract has neither `markConversation` nor an `isMarked` setter field. Do not emulate it through `ex` or local-only state. Use a supported commercial backend capability and then refresh conversations/groups, or disable the UI.
