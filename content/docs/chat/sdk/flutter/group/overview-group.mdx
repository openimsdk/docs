In OpenIM, multi-user chat channels use Groups to govern shared metadata, member rosters, and permissions. The `groupID` uniquely identifies a group and is required for sending group messages, querying group details, managing member rosters, and handling join applications. In the current user's conversation list, the chat channel is identified and managed by `conversationID`; do not conflate the two identifiers.

## Core data structures

| Structure | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GroupInfo` | Group name, avatar, announcement, owner, verification mode, member count, and extensions. |
| `GroupMembersInfo` | A member's in-group nickname, role, mute status, and join details. |
| `GroupApplicationInfo` | Join application records and resolution statuses. |

Group roles are defined in `GroupRoleLevel`: Ordinary members are `20`, administrators are `60`, and group owners are `100`. UI views can toggle actions based on these levels, but final authorization is verified by OpenIMServer.

## State synchronization

Group-related notifications encompass joining groups, leaving groups, group profile updates, and group dissolutions. Register callbacks centrally within the application's single `OnGroupListener`. Use `groupID` as the merge key to update local group stores.

For member events, see [Retrieving group members overview](/sdk/flutter/group/retrieving-group-members/overview-retrieving-group-members); for application events, see [Group applications overview](/sdk/flutter/group/group-applications/overview-group-applications). Future completion, event delivery, and query recalibration are distinct operational phases.

The OpenIM Flutter SDK focuses on in-group metadata, member rosters, and permissions. Public group discovery and large-scale group capacity governance are coordinated between your business backend and OpenIMServer.
