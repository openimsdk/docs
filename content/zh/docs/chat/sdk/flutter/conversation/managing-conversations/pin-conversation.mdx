---
title: '置顶或取消置顶会话'
description: '使用 Flutter SDK 设置指定会话的置顶状态。'
sourcePath: '/sdk/flutter/conversation/managing-conversations/pin-conversation'
---

```dart
await OpenIM.iMManager.conversationManager.setConversation(
  conversationID,
  ConversationReq(isPinned: true),
);
```

`true` 表示置顶，`false` 表示取消置顶。该状态只影响当前用户的会话列表。

Future 成功不等于 `onConversationChanged` 已经到达。后续按 `conversationID` 合并最新会话，完整 listener 见[获取会话列表](/sdk/flutter/conversation/retrieving-conversations/retrieve-conversation-list)。
